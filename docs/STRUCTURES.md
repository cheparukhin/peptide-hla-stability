# Structural Predictions and Representation Hierarchy

This document details how 3D complex predictions, internal foundation-model trunk tensors, inverse-folding likelihoods, and physics-based energy descriptors were generated and assembled into the representation hierarchy (`R0`–`R5`, `full_interface_pos`, `P`, `C`, and `E`), matching [`pepstab/features.py`](../pepstab/features.py) and [`pepstab/round2_representations.py`](../pepstab/round2_representations.py).

---

## 1. Boltz-2 Complex Structure Prediction

### 1.1 Three-Chain Heterotrimer Input and Execution
Every unique `(allele, peptide)` record in [`data/rasmussen_et_al_dataset.csv`](../data/rasmussen_et_al_dataset.csv) ($28,166$ complexes across $75$ HLA alleles and $5,633$ 9-mer peptides) was modeled as a complete three-chain pMHC-I heterotrimer:
- **Chain A (HLA heavy-chain ectodomain)**: Full mature extracellular heavy chain (`data/hla75_constructs.fasta`, whose first $182$ residues form the $\alpha_1\alpha_2$ peptide-binding groove `hla_seq` and preserve the engineered `C67S` substitution for the three mutated constructs). In an initial pilot (`stage4c`), folding the isolated 182-residue $\alpha_1\alpha_2$ domain without the supporting $\alpha_3$ domain and $\beta_2\text{m}$ destabilized the $\beta$-sheet floor; modeling the full three-chain ectodomain restored structural stability across all alleles.
- **Chain B ($\beta_2$-microglobulin)**: Canonical 99-residue human $\beta_2\text{m}$ sequence.
- **Chain C (Peptide)**: 9-mer ligand sequence.

Precomputed multiple sequence alignments (MSAs) were generated once for the 75 unique heavy-chain sequences and $\beta_2\text{m}$ (`reports/msa_manifest.csv`), while the 9-mer peptide chain was run in single-sequence mode (`msa: empty`) so that cross-chain conditioning reflects the specific peptide sequence. All $28,166$ complexes were folded with **Boltz-2** (`boltz==2.2.1`, 1 recycling iteration, 25 diffusion steps, 1 diffusion sample per complex, $100\%$ completion rate) across 25 parallel Modal A10G GPU containers (`143.6` total A10G GPU-hours, $6.2\text{ h}$ wall-clock time; [`reports/gpu_decision.csv`](../reports/gpu_decision.csv)).

## 2. The Representation Hierarchy (`R0`–`R5` and `full_interface_pos`)

A central finding of our study is that *where* and *at what positional resolution* representations are extracted from a structure predictor determines whether they underperform or outperform sequence baselines. Let $\mathcal{P} = (1,\dots,9)$ index the 9 peptide tokens, $\mathcal{D}_{182} = (1,\dots,182)$ index the mature HLA $\alpha_1\alpha_2$ groove tokens, and $\mathcal{G}_{34} \subset \mathcal{D}_{182}$ denote the 34 canonical NetMHCpan polymorphic contact positions (`[7, 9, 24, 45, 59, 62, 63, 66, 67, 69, 70, 73, 74, 76, 77, 80, 81, 84, 95, 97, 99, 114, 116, 118, 143, 147, 150, 152, 156, 158, 159, 163, 167, 171]`).

### 2.1 Sequence and Coarse 3D Summary Baselines (`R0`, `R1`, `R2`)
- **`R0` (One-hot sequence baseline, 860-d)**: Flattened one-hot encoding of the 9 peptide residues and 34 polymorphic HLA contact groove residues ($43 \times 20 = 860$). Used for architecture-matched `FeatureHead` neural and ridge comparisons against structural representations. (Note: our `original_seq_mlp` baseline averages 15 one-hot MLPs and 15 BLOSUM62/5 MLPs, each taking an $860\text{-d}$ input vector; [`pepstab/features.py`](../pepstab/features.py).)
- **`R1` (Coarse 3D geometry and confidence summaries, 50-d; `stage4c5` / `stage5`)**: Extracted from the final diffused 3D coordinates and confidence heads:
  - Per-peptide-position heavy-atom contact counts ($<4.0\,\text{\AA}$ to HLA heavy chain, 9 features) and minimum heavy-atom distances to the HLA groove (9 features).
  - Global interface contact count, peptide radius of gyration, peptide end-to-end distance, and buried surface proxy.
  - Boltz-2 confidence metrics: per-peptide-position pLDDT (9 features), mean peptide pLDDT, complex pLDDT, pTM, ipTM, and peptide–groove interface PAE summaries.
  - *Outcome & Phase D diagnostics*: On the held-out test split, adding `R1` to sequence degraded within-allele Spearman $\rho$ from $0.7064$ to **$0.5947$** ($\Delta\rho = -0.1117$). Multi-seed variance partitioning ([`reports/further_research/phase_d_pose_reliability.csv`](../reports/further_research/phase_d_pose_reliability.csv)) showed that a single Boltz-2 seed already captures a median **$99.1\%$** of within-allele variance across all 109 geometric and confidence features, and L2-shrunk linear residual models on top of frozen sequence predictions ([`reports/further_research/phase_d_residual_selection_phase_a.csv`](../reports/further_research/phase_d_residual_selection_phase_a.csv)) improved 5-fold CV $\rho$ by at most **$+0.0084$**. Coarse 3D descriptors fail because all 9-mer ligands share a nearly identical canonical backbone trace inside the MHC cleft, so scalar distances and confidence summaries discard side-chain chemical identity.
- **`R2` (Chain-pooled Boltz-2 trunk single states, 1,152-d)**: Concatenates the full-chain mean of the frozen Boltz-2 trunk single representation ($s \in \mathbb{R}^{L \times 384}$) across Chain A (HLA ectodomain), Chain B ($\beta_2\text{m}$), and Chain C (9-mer peptide): $3 \times 384 = \mathbf{1,152}$ features.
  - *Outcome*: Collapses to **0.4219** (`R2_mlp`) and **0.3694** (`R2_ridge`) in nested 5-fold CV because averaging across the 9 peptide residues erases which amino acid occupies primary anchor pockets P2 and P9.

### 2.2 Position-Preserving Boltz-2 Trunk Representations (`R3`, `R4`, `R5`, `full_interface_pos`)
To preserve residue-level interface organization while bypassing coordinate diffusion, we extract deterministic internal representations directly from the frozen Boltz-2 trunk single state $s \in \mathbb{R}^{L \times 384}$ and directed pairwise representation $z \in \mathbb{R}^{L \times L \times 128}$:
- **`R3` (Projected positional single-state tokens, 1,528-d) and `R3_pooled_matched` (1,528-d)**: Concatenates the 9 peptide single-state tokens and 182 HLA $\alpha_1\alpha_2$ groove single-state tokens ($191 \times 384$) and projects each token to $8$ channels via a fixed orthonormal QR projection ($Q_{384 \times 8}$, seed `20261012`), yielding $191 \times 8 = \mathbf{1,528}$ features. The dimension-matched control `R3_pooled_matched` tiles the peptide mean vector across the 9 peptide slots and the groove mean vector across the 182 groove slots before applying the identical projection $Q_{384 \times 8}$.
  - *Controlled pooling ablation*: `R3_mlp` achieves **0.6552** vs **0.3165** for `R3_pooled_matched_mlp`, a paired gain of **$\Delta\rho = +0.3387$** ($[+0.3029, +0.3773]$).
- **`R4` (Full peptide-position groove-averaged pair representation, 2,304-d)**: For each of the 9 peptide positions $p \in \{1,\dots,9\}$, we extract both directed 128-channel trunk pair vectors between peptide position $p$ and all 182 HLA $\alpha_1\alpha_2$ groove positions $g \in \mathcal{D}_{182}$, averaged uniformly over the 182 groove residues:
  $$z_{p,\text{pep}\to\text{groove}} = \frac{1}{182}\sum_{g \in \mathcal{D}_{182}} z_{p,g} \in \mathbb{R}^{128}, \qquad z_{p,\text{groove}\to\text{pep}} = \frac{1}{182}\sum_{g \in \mathcal{D}_{182}} z_{g,p} \in \mathbb{R}^{128}.$$
  Concatenating both directions across all 9 peptide positions yields $9 \times 2 \times 128 = \mathbf{2,304}$ features.
  - *Outcome*: `R4_ridge` reaches **0.6845**, `R4_mlp` reaches **0.7136**, and blending `R4_ridge` with the sequence ensemble (`blend_seq_R4`) reaches **0.7238** in nested 5-fold CV.
- **`R5` (Anchor-resolved contact-pooled pair representation, 1,024-d) and `R5_shuffled_contact` (1,024-d)**: Slices the trunk pair tensor to the $9 \times 34$ interface between peptide positions $p \in \mathcal{P}$ and the 34 polymorphic contact positions $g \in \mathcal{G}_{34}$. For each peptide position $p$, a binary crystal contact mask $M_p \subset \mathcal{G}_{34}$ is defined as the union of groove residues within heavy-atom distance $\le 4.5\,\text{\AA}$ of peptide position $p$ across five reference pMHC-I crystal structures (`1X7Q`, `1XR9`, `4QRU`, `5N6B`, `7LFZ`; yielding subset sizes $|M_{1\dots 9}| = [9, 12, 11, 6, 10, 6, 5, 5, 11]$). Defining the contact-averaged bidirectional pair vector $\bar{u}_p = \frac{1}{|M_p|}\sum_{g \in M_p} [z_{p,g};\, z_{g,p}] \in \mathbb{R}^{2 \times 128}$, `R5` preserves primary/auxiliary anchors **P1**, **P2**, and **P9** at single-residue resolution and averages the central bulge **P3–P8** with equal weight ($4 \times 2 \times 128 = \mathbf{1,024}$ features).
  - *Outcome & shuffled-contact ablation*: `R5_mlp` reaches **0.7210** in nested 5-fold CV, **0.5667** on unseen HLA groups ($\Delta\rho = +0.4062$ over `R0_mlp`), and **0.4980** on joint HLA-group $\times$ peptide-cluster holdouts ($\Delta\rho = +0.3738$). Permuting the 34 groove columns in the contact mask $M$ with a fixed random seed (`20261012`) to form `R5_shuffled_contact_mlp` yields **0.7203** ($\Delta\rho = +0.0007$ $[-0.0096, +0.0236]$), proving that anchor position resolution—not the specific crystal contact mask—drives the gain.
- **`full_interface_pos` (Unpooled $9 \times 34$ peptide–groove pair interface, 2,448-d) and `full_interface_pooled` (2,448-d)**: Retains all $9 \times 34 = 306$ peptide–contact-groove residue pairs by projecting each 128-channel direction of $(z_{p,g}, z_{g,p})$ to $4$ channels via a fixed orthonormal QR projection ($Q_{128 \times 4}$, seed `20261012`), yielding $9 \times 34 \times 2 \times 4 = \mathbf{2,448}$ features. The dimension-matched control `full_interface_pooled` averages those projected vectors over $M_p$ per peptide position and tiles them across the 34 groove columns ($2,448\text{-d}$).
  - *Outcome*: Achieves the highest single-model score in nested 5-fold CV (**0.7315**), outperforming `full_interface_pooled_mlp` (**0.5653**) by **$\Delta\rho = +0.1663$** ($[+0.1378, +0.1992]$).

## 3. Secondary Structural Descriptors: ProteinMPNN (`P`, `C`) and FoldX (`E`)

To test whether explicit inverse-folding likelihoods or empirical force-field energies add complementary information beyond `R5` trunk pair features, we extracted two additional descriptor families ([`reports/followup3/`](../reports/followup3/)):

1. **ProteinMPNN native-peptide log-likelihoods (`P` and `C`, 13 + 13 features)**:
   - Using fixed-backbone ProteinMPNN (`v_48_020`), we scored the log-likelihood of the native 9-mer peptide sequence in two structural contexts:
     - **Peptide-only context (`P`, 13 features)**: Conditioned solely on the predicted 9-mer peptide backbone coordinates (total, mean, core P3–P8, anchor P1/P2/P9, and all 9 individual positional log-likelihoods).
     - **Full complex context (`C`, 13 features)**: Conditioned on the complete pMHC-I heterotrimer backbone and fixed HLA + $\beta_2\text{m}$ sequences, capturing pocket-specific inverse-folding compatibility.
   - Evaluated on a balanced 4,449-pair subset ($69$ alleles; [`reports/followup3/inverse_execution_v2/comparisons.csv`](../reports/followup3/inverse_execution_v2/comparisons.csv)) and scaled to all **19,716** development complexes ([`reports/followup3/inverse_fulltrain_candidate/comparisons.csv`](../reports/followup3/inverse_fulltrain_candidate/comparisons.csv)), `R5+P+C` showed no gain over `R5` alone on the full cohort (**0.6885** vs **0.6955**, $\Delta\rho = -0.0070$ $[-0.0220, +0.0131]$).

2. **Repaired FoldX empirical energy descriptors (`E`, 29 features)**:
   - On the balanced 4,449-pair subset, each predicted complex was relaxed with `FoldX RepairPDB` and scored with `AnalyseComplex` and `Stability` to extract 29 thermodynamic terms ($\Delta G_{\text{interaction}}$, van der Waals, electrostatics, polar and apolar solvation, hydrogen-bond energetics, side-chain and main-chain entropy, electrostatic $k_{\text{on}}$, and intra-/inter-chain clash penalties).
   - Adding `E` to `R5` (`R5+E` vs `R5`; [`reports/followup3/energy_execution_v2/comparisons.csv`](../reports/followup3/energy_execution_v2/comparisons.csv)) yielded $0.6157$ vs $0.6007$ ($\Delta\rho = +0.0150$, $98.33\%$ Bonferroni CI $[-0.0524, +0.0450]$), providing no established improvement over `R5` alone.

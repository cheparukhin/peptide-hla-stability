When and why do structural foundation models outperform sequence predictors for pMHC-I kinetic stability?

T-cell recognition requires a peptide–HLA class I complex to persist at the cell surface long enough to engage surveying T-cell receptors. Dissociation half-life (t1/2 = ln 2 / koff) is a kinetic property distinct from equilibrium binding affinity (Kd = koff / kon; median within-allele Spearman ρ = 0.580 across 7,281 dual-labeled pairs). Using the Rasmussen et al. (2016) scintillation proximity assay dataset (28,166 complexes across 5,633 9-mer peptides and 75 HLA alleles) and 897,600 evaluated pairs from the MHC Motif Atlas, we dissect where sequence ensembles suffice, why naive foundation-model summaries fail, and how internal pair representations from the Boltz-2 trunk capture transferable peptide–groove coupling.

01 · Sequence Baselines, Elution Transfer & Naive Foundation Models

Sequence ensembles transfer zero-shot to natural ligand presentation, whereas coarse 3D summaries fail

On the held-out peptide-cluster test split (5,565 pairs across 67 eligible alleles), a 30-model multilayer perceptron (MLP) ensemble taking the 9-mer peptide and 34-residue HLA contact pseudosequence achieves a median within-allele Spearman correlation of 0.7064, outperforming a single network by +0.0883 (0.6181) and matching a full 182-residue HLA domain ensemble (0.6904). Replacing or augmenting the sequence inputs with frozen ESM-2 35M embeddings yields no gain (0.6979, Δρ = −0.0085), and compressing 28,166 predicted Boltz-2 3D structures (143.6 A10G GPU-hours) into 50 hand-engineered pocket contact counts, minimum distances, and confidence scalars (pLDDT, ipTM, PAE) actively degrades ranking to 0.5947 (Δρ = −0.1117).

Across 140 overlapping peptide–HLA pairs shared between the Rasmussen et al. kinetic assay and the MHC Motif Atlas, naturally eluted ligands exhibit a 7.2-fold longer median half-life than non-eluted measured peptides (7.90 h vs 1.10 h, p = 6.0 × 10−31) and a 10-fold lower zero-stability floor rate (2.1% vs 21.2%). Position-preserving trunk pair representations beat competitive sequence predictors

Coarse 3D summaries fail because all 9-mer peptides share a nearly invariant backbone trace inside the MHC cleft, so distance cutoffs and chain-level pooling (R2, ρ = 0.4219) discard residue-specific anchor compatibility while introducing diffusion-seed coordinate jitter. Extracting deterministic, position-preserving peptide–groove pair representations directly from the frozen Boltz-2 trunk (R4: 9 × 2 × 128 = 2,304-d; R5: 4 × 2 × 128 = 1,024-d keeping anchors P1, P2, P9 separate from P3–P8) lifts nested 5-fold cross-validation performance on the 19,716-pair training cohort from 0.6668 (for a competitive sequence selector spanning standard and allele-centered 30-MLP ensembles, gradient-boosted trees, and pairwise rankers) to 0.7212 (Δρ = +0.0543 [+0.0314, +0.0808]). Blending a linear R4 ridge head with the sequence ensemble reaches 0.7238 (+0.0569 [+0.0426, +0.0838]).

Controlled ablations isolate the mechanism: preserving positional token identity outperforms dimension-matched pooled trunk controls by +0.3387 [+0.3029, +0.3773] in projected space (R3: 0.6552 vs 0.3165) and by +0.1663 [+0.1378, +0.1992] across the full 9 × 34 interface (0.7315 vs 0.5653). By contrast, pooling HLA residues via a crystal-derived contact mask (R5, 0.7210) provides no advantage over randomly shuffled HLA groove subsets (0.7203, Δρ = +0.0007 [−0.0096, +0.0236]), because trunk self-attention has already propagated pocket context across the groove tokens.

03 · Unseen HLA Sequence Groups & Label Efficiency

Trunk representations generalize across held-out HLA groups where sequence models collapse

To test generalization across the binding groove, we grouped all 75 HLA alleles into 5 folds by contact-pseudosequence Hamming distance ≤ 3 and crossed them with the 5 peptide-cluster folds. When target HLA groups are excluded from supervised fitting, sequence predictors collapse (ρ = 0.1605 for matched MLPs; 0.1921 for the full sequence selector), whereas Boltz-2 R5 representations maintain strong within-allele ranking across both unseen HLA groups and jointly unseen peptide clusters.

04 · Few-Shot Allele Calibration

Twenty measurements per target allele calibrate frozen trunk representations

Under strict joint holdout across 68 eligible alleles, adapting a frozen Boltz-2 R5 ridge predictor with 20 measured support peptides from the target allele raises median within-allele Spearman ρ from 0.5296 (zero-shot) to 0.5763—a paired gain of +0.0468 (pointwise 95% crossed CI [+0.0227, +0.1035]; 3-primary Bonferroni-adjusted 98.33% CI [+0.0140, +0.1195]). Because within-allele rank correlation is invariant to additive shifts, this improvement requires rotating the feature weights (+0.0444 [+0.0165, +0.0928] over a scalar-offset-only control, which changes ρ by just +0.0024 [−0.0062, +0.0239]).

05 · Secondary & Negative Investigations

06 · Discussion, Scope & Prospective Validation

Why internal trunk representations succeed—and what prospective validation must test

Our findings resolve an apparent contradiction in applying structural foundation models to peptide–MHC complexes. Because all 9-mer ligands adopt a nearly identical backbone trace inside the MHC class I binding cleft, summarizing predicted 3D structures via coarse contact counts or chain-pooled embeddings erases the alignment between peptide anchor positions (P1, P2, P9) and polymorphic HLA pockets while injecting stochastic coordinate noise from diffusion sampling. Interrogating the deterministic Boltz-2 trunk pair tensor directly at each peptide position preserves the learned 128-channel biophysical compatibility of the interface, enabling a simple linear or shallow MLP head to outperform tuned sequence ensembles in-panel (+0.0543) and to generalize across unseen HLA sequence groups (+0.35 to +0.41).

Operationally, these results support a two-tier screening architecture: the 30-MLP sequence ensemble executes in 0.073 s per 1,000 complexes on CPU (achieving ρ = 0.7064 on held-out test peptides and 0.9656 AUROC on proteome-wide ligand elution), whereas Boltz-2 R4/R5 trunk representations (143.6 A10G GPU-hours for 28,166 complexes) provide superior accuracy for focused lead prioritization, sequence+structure blending (ρ = 0.7238), and under-characterized or novel HLA alleles.

Several scope boundaries apply. Supervised stability labels derive from a single scintillation proximity assay measuring β2-microglobulin dissociation at 37 °C without replicate-level decay curves, and Boltz-2 was pre-trained on public structural databases containing MHC crystal structures. Bootstrap intervals quantify sampling variability across peptide clusters and HLA groups conditional on the evaluation cohort. The decisive next step is a prospective, blinded wet-lab dissociation study across held-out HLA alleles and de novo peptide clusters using the released frozen models (prospective assay protocol).

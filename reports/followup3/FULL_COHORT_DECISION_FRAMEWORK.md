# Blind full-cohort expansion decision framework

Recorded 13 October 2026, before this reviewer opened inverse or energy
efficacy results. The recommendation was sent to Root before result downloads
were confirmed. Root will wait for both frozen studies and their independent
audits before selecting at most one expansion under the user's authorization.

Prioritize an increment over the matched R5 MLP, not standalone compatibility
or energy correlations. For ProteinMPNN, require a coherent positive net
R5+P+C minus R5 result; a positive context primary that merely repairs a harmful
P-only feature is insufficient. A positive family interval on the primary and
a positive net increment provide the strongest rationale. A positive but
uncertain net result can justify a coverage/training-density study if its
possible benefit merits the cost, but cannot be presented as rescuing a null
or as independent confirmation. Avoid expansion for a negative/negligible
increment with little plausible useful upside, or a result dependent on one
allele/fold.

ProteinMPNN is the default economical candidate if it passes that evidence
gate: approximately $23 additional feature extraction was forecast. Full
FoldX should require clearly stronger incremental evidence given its estimated
$65–80 additional cost and much slower per-pair extraction. Its admission must
wait for existing reservations to settle. Do not spend merely because credits
remain; if neither candidate earns expansion, stop below the ceiling.

The minimal expansion uses exactly the original 19,716 training rows and
unchanged peptide folds and targets. Reuse the 4,449 verified feature rows and
extract only the 15,267 additional pairs under matching provenance. Freeze
one R5+selected-feature versus R5 comparison, repeating the same two-member
hidden-64, three-L2, three-inner/five-outer procedure: 200 MLP fits for two
arms. A predeclared ridge sensitivity adds 100 fits if justified. Do not add
a new grid, combined feature arm, cutoff search or second feature search.
Reuse earlier full-cohort baseline artifacts only if fitting, stopping, tuning,
features and recipes match exactly; subset baselines cannot be reused.

Keep subset and full-cohort results separate. Do not pool or average their
out-of-fold predictions. The subset informed feature choice, and the added
rows contain previously available development labels: expansion measures
coverage/training-density transport, not new independent assay evidence.
Report every frozen outcome, including nulls and failures.

Resource admission must use hard bounds, not the above forecasts. At the
decision, Root reports $21.178632 conservatively closed and $40 reserved for
energy extraction, leaving $58.821368 under the $120 planned commitment cap.
The absolute ceiling stays $139 with operational reserve retained. No paid
expansion is admitted by this framework.

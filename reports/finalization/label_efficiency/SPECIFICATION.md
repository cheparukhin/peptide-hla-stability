# Fixed-head label-budget sensitivity on R0 and R5

This is a bounded conditional development study on the canonical 19,716 original training rows, 3,587 peptide clusters and five existing peptide outer folds. It asks whether the matched R5−R0 performance gap is larger with 25% of the outer-fitting peptide clusters than at 100%. It adds no representation extraction, head selection, hyperparameter tuning, trees, validation/test access or prospective assay experiment. The standard heads were nominated using full-data development history; this design cannot establish prospective assay savings.

Use the existing frozen R0 positional peptide/pseudosequence one-hot and R5 contact-pooled Boltz features. Restore the exact canonical original-training target bits through the approved existing cloud adapter and validate the unchanged hybrid population, feature, source and protocol identities. Root must freeze the new specification, script, task manifest, baseline-report certificate and source hashes before any new fit. A freeze is not an execution authorization.

## Label-blind nested budgets

For draws 0, 1 and 2, order integer peptide-cluster IDs by the SHA256 of `pepstab-label-efficiency-v1|draw={draw}|cluster={cluster_id}`, breaking an exact hash tie by cluster ID. These three global orderings are predetermined and do not use labels, representation values or performance. For each outer-fitting partition, rank only its available clusters. Retain the first `ceil(0.25*n_clusters)` or `ceil(0.50*n_clusters)` clusters; 100% retains all. The 25% set is nested in the 50% set, which is nested in 100%. The same sets apply to R0, R5 and both members. Include every canonical measurement belonging to each selected cluster; budget fractions are fractions of clusters, not measurements or alleles.

Construct the five stopping-fold assignments once from the **original full outer-fitting partition**, using unchanged `nested.inner_folds(outer_fit, 5)`. For member `(stop_fold=0, seed=0)` or `(stop_fold=1, seed=1)`, intersect both its original gradient partition and its original stopping partition with the chosen cluster subset. Never recompute stopping assignments after subsampling. Gradient, stopping and outer-assessment clusters remain disjoint. Stopping labels count toward the label budget. Report the union of selected gradient/stopping rows and clusters, as well as each member's gradient/stopping counts, actual row fractions and cluster fractions. Whole-cluster sampling can change allele composition; no outcome-based replenishment or stratification is allowed. Empty gradient/stopping partitions are a failure, not permission to redraw.

## Fixed head and 100% reuse

Each representation uses the unchanged standard head: gradient-only StandardScaler; one hidden ReLU layer of 64; linear scalar output; unweighted MSE on canonical log1p half-life; Adam .001; natural batches 256; L2 .01; at most 200 epochs; stopping-MSE patience 15; restore best stopping weights. Use the two members above, averaging their held predictions arithmetically within each outer-fold/representation/subset draw. No inner selection or calibration is added.

Exactly **120 new supervised MLP fits** are allowed: 2 partial budgets × 3 draws × 5 outer folds × 2 representations × 2 members. These are final outer-held assessments, not an additional nested hyperparameter search. At 100%, reuse exactly the 20 already fitted hybrid `standard` final members, and their existing two-member averaged predictions, only after the original full hybrid report succeeds and is provider-certified. Reconstruct and compare each full member's exact gradient/stopping/held IDs, feature identities, canonical labels, configuration and full bundle identity against the authenticated hybrid artifacts. Check the bound raw-final prediction and bundle/completion hashes. Any mismatch stops the study; it does not authorize a new 100% fit. The same shared 100% reference applies to all three draws; do not count it as three new ensembles or independent observations.

Save every new member's exclusive attempt, selected-row identity, scaler state, model weights/biases/offset, stopping curve, epoch metadata, held predictions and payload hashes. Preserve failed attempts. There is no automatic retry, subset continuation, widening of budget or extra seed.

## Endpoints and uncertainty

For each partial budget and draw, pool the five outer-held blocks and score each representation separately on the fixed full-cohort eligible min20 and min50 panels. The endpoint is median per-allele Spearman across pooled held predictions. Average the **three draw scores** and the three matched draw gaps; never average predictions across subset draws into a larger ensemble. Report every draw score/gap, the mean absolute R0/R5 curves at 25/50/100%, the shared full-data gap, and actual budgets.

The single primary contrast at min20 is:

`mean_draw[R5_25_score − R0_25_score] − [R5_100_score − R0_100_score]`.

Positive values mean the matched representation gap is larger at 25%, conditional on these procedures and subsets. This does not by itself mean that R5 at 25% matches R5 at 100%, or quantify an equivalent number of saved measurements. The 50%-versus-100% gap change and min50 repetitions are secondary sensitivity results; they cannot trigger more fits.

Use the existing `cluster_bootstrap_scores` helper with exactly 2,000 shared whole-peptide-cluster draws and seed 20261003 for all 14 prediction vectors (12 partial-budget/draw/representation vectors plus the two shared full-data references), on each panel. Fix eligibility from full counts/labels, reuse the same draw sequence across panels and preserve existing rank/tie/constant-prediction conventions. Within each bootstrap draw, compute each subset-replicate score first, then average replicate scores/gaps and form the primary difference of gaps. Do not bootstrap three subset draws as if they were independent cohorts, subtract independently computed CI endpoints, or ensemble their predictions. Report percentile 95% intervals and bootstrap-mean-minus-point diagnostics; fail if a whole eligible panel becomes degenerate.

Intervals condition on fixed fitted heads and fixed selected subsets. They omit refitting, stopping-epoch variation, subset-design randomness beyond the three fixed realizations, prior recipe nomination and independent assay variation. Full-data history influenced the choice of representations and recipes. R0/R5 dimensions and parameter counts differ. Allele composition varies with whole-cluster budgets. No result establishes a causal biological mechanism, assay reliability, new-HLA transfer performance or prospective assay savings.

## Bounded execution and purpose

Root owns the minimal Modal execution and conservative ledger. Require a separate source-bound authorization, successful audited hybrid-report predecessor, released provider identity, fixed deadline, two numerical threads and at most the authorized worker count. The **$10 inclusive study cap** covers fitting, feature staging, reporting, storage/egress and shutdown within the existing $100 total ceiling; it is a ceiling rather than a target. No models, inference or bootstrap are run locally. The script adds no scheduler or provider APIs and stops on an existing attempt or failed member.

Existing standard-head telemetry, without inspecting held scores, gives 20 full-data final fits totaling 27.255 seconds. Maximum recorded seconds per epoch were 0.026868 for R0 and 0.033388 for R5. Bounding all 120 new fits by 200 epochs at the slower full-data rate gives about **801 seconds of serial learner time**; a factor-two planning allowance is about **27 minutes**, excluding input/feature preparation, serialization, fixed report bootstrap and provider lifecycle. This is a forecast, not a hard runtime guarantee. Smaller subsets may stop later, which is why the epoch-cap calculation is used. A one-hour work window with separately reserved lifecycle/report allowance is a reasonable admission bound only if Root's complete cost and deadline checks fit the remaining ceiling. Stop rather than expanding if it does not fit.

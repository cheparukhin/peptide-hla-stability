# Stage 1 — data audit and frozen evaluation

Generated by `scripts/audit_data.py`.
Source: `data/rasmussen_et_al_dataset.csv` (read-only), `data/splits.csv` (frozen).

**Key findings.** (1) Dataset is clean — no missing values, no duplicates,
fixed-length fields, standard amino acid alphabet. (2) Two alleles share a
pseudosequence, so pseudosequence-only models can't tell them apart (756 rows).
(3) 20.2% of labels are exactly zero — three lines of evidence say these are
detection-floor censoring, not real measurements. (4) Peptide panels are
allele-specific, which broke a naive split and forced a fix. (5) Test set
resolves Delta Spearman ~ 0.05, setting the improvement bar.

pseudosequence-only model must give identical predictions for both; the
full-domain baseline can distinguish them. Known ceiling, not a data error.

## 2. Engineered constructs

3 of 75 alleles carry a C67S substitution — the cysteine at HLA position 67
replaced with serine because the free cysteine caused the protein to aggregate
in the assay. The substitution is already present in `hla_seq` and
`hla_pseudoseq`, so the sequences describe what was actually measured. No
correction needed.

| Allele | Rows | Zero-label share |
|---|---:|---:|
| `HLA-B*14:01(C67S)` | 374 | 89.0% |
| `HLA-B*14:02(C67S)` | 382 | 74.9% |
| `HLA-B*39:06(C67S)` | 379 | 92.1% |
| *(72 natural alleles)* | 27,031 | 17.4% |

Position 67 is naturally polymorphic (V, S, Y, M, F, C across the 72 natural
alleles), so `S` there doesn't mark a construct on its own. The high zero rates
are consistent with the mutation weakening binding. No matched wild-type alleles
exist in the dataset.

### Zeros: 5,679 rows (20.16%)

Three lines of evidence say these are left-censored at a detection floor:

1. **A gap next to the spike.** Grid counts: 0.0 h = 5,679 | 0.1 h = 443 |
   0.2 h = 1,297 | 0.3 h = 1,289 | 0.4 h = 1,105. A fit over 0.2–1.0 h
   predicts ~1,651 at 0.1 h; only 443 observed. A smooth distribution can't
   have a dip right above its mode — values below ~0.1–0.2 h were recorded as 0.

2. **Zeros track weak binding.** Across 68 alleles with >= 50 rows, zero share
   anti-correlates with median non-zero label (Spearman rho = -0.608,
   p = 3.7e-08). Alleles that hold peptides poorly produce more zeros — expected
   from a floor, not from random dropout.

3. **Spread across peptides.** Among 746 peptides on >= 10 alleles, only 2 are
   zero everywhere, 99 are never zero. Median per-peptide zero share is 0.20.

No pile-up at the top: max 256.7 h, 56 rows > 100 h, mostly distinct values.

**Decision.** Zeros are detection-floor censoring — half-lives too short to
resolve.

- Keep them (20% of data, real signal: "dissociates fast").
- Train on `log1p(thalf_hours)` (defined at 0, maps floor to 0).
- Lead with rank metrics — Spearman handles the tied block cleanly. MAE at the
  floor is biased both ways against recorded labels (see EVALUATION.md).
- Don't read sub-floor predictions as measurements.
- Tobit-style censored regression would be principled but is out of scope.

Median nearest-neighbour distance: 4 substitutions. 871 peptides (15.5%) have a
neighbour within 3 — sparse enough that grouping at Hamming <= 3 costs almost
nothing, but the near-duplicate minority is what a model could memorise.

Each peptide was tested on a median of 4 alleles (range 1–36); 1,692 appear on
one allele only. The measured grid is 6.7% full, because each allele has its own
peptide panel. This is why splits must be allele-aware: cluster distribution
controls per-allele held-out coverage.

### Per-allele coverage

74 of 75 alleles in all three splits. Per-allele test share: median 0.201,
IQR [0.191, 0.206]. 67 alleles have >= 50 test rows (evaluation-eligible).

7 have <= 50 rows total — rare in the assay, not a clustering artifact.

**Correction to the plan.** The plan predicted 15 missing alleles and 54
clearing 50 test rows. Those numbers came from a water-fill ranked by absolute
deficit, which degenerates: once deficits equalise, clusters cycle round-robin
and large clusters always land in whichever split led early. With
allele-specific panels, this left ~350-row alleles with 0 test rows
(`HLA-B*42:01`, `HLA-B*51:01`, `HLA-B*81:01`). Ranking by deficit relative to
the target fixes it, no cost to 70/10/20 totals or distance guarantee.

## 6. Evaluation resolution

67 eligible test alleles covering 98.8% of test rows (5,565 / 5,633).

- Shuffled labels: median rho = 0.000 +/- 0.018, 95% range [-0.036, +0.035].
- Paired bootstrap under no true difference: CI half-width 0.037–0.053.

**Delta Spearman = 0.05** is the smallest difference this test set can separate
from noise. That's the predeclared improvement bar.
